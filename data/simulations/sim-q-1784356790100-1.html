Clause 7.6.4: Angle of Inclination

Lacing bars shall be inclined at an angle not less than 40° and not more than 70° to the axis of the member.

⚠️

Clause 7.6.1.1: Transverse Shear

Lacing systems shall be designed to resist a transverse shear force, V = 2.5% of the axial force on the member.

WELCOME TO THE LACING LAB

A Steel Structure Design Playground

This high-fidelity educational simulation models back-to-back channel steel columns subjected to high axial compression force. Your goal is to connect them with a lacing system compliant with Indian Standard (IS 800:2007).

How to Play & Learn:

  • Drag the Single Lacing Flat from the left dock and drop it onto the main central workspace.
  • Use the sliders in the sidebars to alter Axial Load, Angle, and Spacing dynamically.
  • Watch the real-time physical indicators monitor compliance with critical structural regulations.
  • Sweep your mouse across the central column, clicking and holding to apply lateral shear load and simulate live buckling!
