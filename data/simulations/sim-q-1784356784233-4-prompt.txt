## 3. PHYSICAL MATHEMATICS ENGINE (IS 800:2007)
The model must implement the real-time structural calculator engine in JavaScript:
- **Angle Dimensions**:
  - Connected Leg $b_1 = 100 \text{ mm}$
  - Outstanding Leg $b_2 = 100 \text{ mm}$
  - Thickness $t = 10 \text{ mm}$
  - Gross Area $A_g = (b_1 + b_2 - t) \times t = 1900 \text{ mm}^2$
- **Yielding Strength ($T_{dg}$)**:
  $$T_{dg} = \frac{A_g \cdot f_y}{\gamma_{m0}} \times 10^{-3} \text{ (kN)}$$
  Where $\gamma_{m0} = 1.1$.
- **Rupture Strength with Shear Lag ($T_{dn}$)**:
  - Connected leg area $A_{nc} = (b_1 - t/2) \times t = 950 \text{ mm}^2$
  - Outstanding leg area $A_{go} = (b_2 - t/2) \times t = 950 \text{ mm}^2$
  - Shear lag width $w = b_2 = 100 \text{ mm}$
  - Shear lag distance $b_s = w = 100 \text{ mm}$ (for welded connection)
  - Shear lag factor $\beta$:
    $$\beta = 1.4 - 0.076 \times \left(\frac{w}{t}\right) \times \left(\frac{f_y}{f_u}\right) \times \left(\frac{b_s}{L_w}\right)$$
    *Constraint:* $0.7 \le \beta \le \frac{f_u \cdot \gamma_{m0}}{f_y \cdot \gamma_{m1}}$ where $\gamma_{m1} = 1.25$.
  - Rupture Capacity:
    $$T_{dn} = \left( \frac{0.9 \cdot A_{nc} \cdot f_u}{\gamma_{m1}} + \frac{\beta \cdot A_{go} \cdot f_y}{\gamma_{m0}} \right) \times 10^{-3} \text{ (kN)}$$
- **Governing Tensile Capacity ($T_d$)**:
  $$T_d = \min(T_{dg}, T_{dn})$$
