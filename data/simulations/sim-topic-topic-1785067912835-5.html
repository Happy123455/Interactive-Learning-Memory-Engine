IS 800 : 2007 Codebook

Clause 7.7 - Design of Battened Members

Cl. 7.7.1.1 — Number of Battens

The compression member must be divided into at least three bays by battens. This ensures complete system constraint.

Cl. 7.7.1.4 — Spacing of Battens

The local slenderness ratio of a column component (λlocal) between continuous battens must not be greater than 50 or 0.7 times the overall maximum slenderness ratio of the whole member.

Cl. 7.7.2.3 — Thickness of Plates

The thickness (t) of continuous batten plates shall not be less than 1/50 of the distance (d) between the innermost connecting lines of bolts or welding lines.

Cl. 7.7.2.1 — Width of Plates

End battens shall have an effective depth (d) of not less than the distance between centroids of members, and intermediate battens not less than 3/4 of that distance.
