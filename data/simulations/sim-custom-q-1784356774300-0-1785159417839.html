📖 IS 800:2007 Codebook Clauses

Interactive design rules for Indian Standard Code of Practice for General Construction in Steel.

Clause 10.3.2 Clearances

Standard clearance hole size $d_0 = d + 2\text{mm}$ for bolts up to M24.

Clause 10.2.2 Minimum Pitch

Pitch distance shall not be less than 2.5 × nominal diameter of the bolt ($p \ge 2.5d$).

Clause 10.2.4 Edge Distance

Minimum edge distance $e \ge 1.7 \times d_0$ for hand flame-cut edges, or $1.5 \times d_0$ for machine sheared edges.

Clause 10.3.5 Block Shear Failure

Block shear strength is governed by tearing along a plane of shear and tension yielding across an perpendicular plane.
