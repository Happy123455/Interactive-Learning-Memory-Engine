## 4. MATHEMATICAL ENGINE & REAL-TIME ENGINEERING CODE CALCULATIONS
Implement the strict formulations from **IS 800:2007 (Indian Standard Code of Practice for General Construction in Steel)**:

### A. Bolt Hole Clearance & Ultimate Tensile Strength
*   Based on Bolt Diameter ($d$):
    *   If $d \in [12, 14]$ mm, clearance $\Delta = 1.0$ mm.
    *   If $d \in [16, 24]$ mm, clearance $\Delta = 2.0$ mm.
    *   If $d > 24$ mm, clearance $\Delta = 3.0$ mm.
    *   Hole diameter $d_0 = d + \Delta$.
*   Ultimate Tensile Strength of Bolt ($f_{ub}$):
    *   For Bolt Class 4.6: $f_{ub} = 400 \text{ MPa}$, yield strength $f_{yb} = 240 \text{ MPa}$.
    *   For Bolt Class 8.8: $f_{ub} = 800 \text{ MPa}$, yield strength $f_{yb} = 640 \text{ MPa}$.
    *   For Bolt Class 10.9: $f_{ub} = 1000 \text{ MPa}$, yield strength $f_{yb} = 900 \text{ MPa}$.
*   Ultimate Strength of Plate ($f_u$): Assume Fe 410 grade, $f_u = 410 \text{ MPa}$, yield strength $f_y = 250 \text{ MPa}$.

### B. Shear Capacity of Black Bolt ($V_{dsb}$) - IS 800, Cl 10.3.3
$$V_{dsb} = \frac{V_{nsb}}{\gamma_{mb}}$$
Where partial safety factor $\gamma_{mb} = 1.25$.
$$V_{nsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\sqrt{3}} \left( n_n A_{nb} + n_s A_{sb} \right)$$
*   $A_{sb} = \frac{\pi}{4} d^2$ (shank area).
*   $A_{nb} \approx 0.78 A_{sb}$ (threaded area).
*   For Lap Joint: $n_n = 1, n_s = 0$ (single shear plane passes through threads).
*   For Double Cover Butt Joint: $n_n = 1, n_s = 1$ (double shear plane: one in threads, one in shank).

### C. Bearing Capacity of Black Bolt ($V_{dpb}$) - IS 800, Cl 10.3.4
$$V_{dpb} = \frac{V_{npb}}{\gamma_{mb}} = \frac{2.5 \, k_b \, d \, t \, f_u}{1.25}$$
Where $k_b$ is the minimum of:
1.  $\frac{e}{3 d_0}$
2.  $\frac{p}{3 d_0} - 0.25$
3.  $\frac{f_{ub}}{f_u}$
4.  $1.0$
*   $t$ is the sum of thickness of plates experiencing bearing in the same direction (for lap joint, minimum of plate thicknesses; for double cover butt joint, minimum of main plate thickness vs. sum of cover plate thicknesses).

### D. Slip Resistance of HSFG Bolt ($V_{dsf}$) - IS 800, Cl 10.4.3
$$V_{dsf} = \frac{V_{nsf}}{\gamma_{mf}}$$
Where partial safety factor $\gamma_{mf} = 1.25$.
$$V_{nsf} = \mu_f \, n_e \, K_h \, F_0$$
*   Slip factor (friction coefficient) $\mu_f = 0.48$ (assuming sand-blasted surface).
*   $n_e = 1$ (for single shear lap) or $2$ (for double cover butt).
*   $K_h = 1.0$ (for normal round holes).
*   Proof load $F_0 = A_{nb} \cdot f_{0}$, where pre-tension stress $f_{0} = 0.7 f_{ub}$.
