Contrast of Tone Definition

Contrast of Tone is the juxtaposition of distinct light and dark values. Without it, the visual system experiences monotony, failing to establish spatial hierarchy, architectural form boundaries, or depth.

Vdiff = ΔLavg * (1 + σscale) * sin(θ)
L: Luminance variance | σ: Scale Disparity | θ: Angular Tension
※ Core Theorem: Visual difference defines clarity.
