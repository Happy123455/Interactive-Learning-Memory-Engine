Compacted Subgrade Layer 4

Direct support foundation of the pavement. Compaction improves the Subgrade Modulus (k-value) preventing localized settling.

Dry Lean Concrete Layer 3

75-100mm thick base layer providing uniform, erosion-resistant support. Acts as an excellent working platform for pavers.

Separation Membrane Layer 2

A 125-micron polythene sheet. Essential to reduce friction between PQC and DLC, preventing cracks and cement slurry loss.

PQC Concrete Slab Layer 1

High Flexural Grade structural layer (typically M40+). Absorbs load stress, bending action, and weather extremes.

Studio Manual & Theory

Welcome to the Rigid Pavement Construction Studio. This digital simulation models structural stresses, subgrade modulus performance, and geological sequencing following IRC:58 standard design procedures.

How to Play & Design:

  • Assemble the Stack: Concrete slabs must rest on strong foundations. Drag the 4 materials on the left and position them in proper geological order: Compacted Subgrade (Base), DLC Sub-base, Separation Membrane, and PQC structural slab.
  • Perform Compliance Audits: Complete the IS Code Challenge snippets. Answering correctly boosts your Design Score and compliance levels.
  • X-Ray Inspection: Enable the X-Ray Scanner in the center panel HUD to inspect internal transverse structural joints, dowel bars, tie bars, and material matrices.
  • Optimization Sliders: Once constructed, vary PQC thickness and soil foundation properties. Observe the mechanical stress response mapped real-time on the stress-profile curve.
