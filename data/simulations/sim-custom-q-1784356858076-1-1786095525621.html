Desirable Properties of Paving Bitumen

1. Fluid Consistency & Viscosity

Bitumen must possess adequate fluid viscosity at elevated mixing temperatures ($135^\circ\text{C}-160^\circ\text{C}$) to thoroughly coat aggregate particles. At summer pavement temperatures ($60^\circ\text{C}$), it must retain high shear resistance to prevent permanent pavement deformation (rutting).

2. Low Temperature Susceptibility

Bitumen is thermoplastic. A high-quality binder exhibits minimal variation in stiffness across extreme temperature ranges. This is quantified by the Penetration Index ($PI$). A low temperature susceptibility prevents brittle thermal cracking during winter freeze.

3. Strong Aggregate Adhesion

Bitumen must wet and adhere strongly to mineral aggregates. Excellent interfacial tension prevents moisture damage (stripping), where water displaces the bitumen film from hydrophilic aggregate surfaces.

4. Long-Term Durability

Resists weathering and oxidative hardening caused by atmospheric oxygen and UV radiation, preserving binder ductility and fatigue life over decades.
