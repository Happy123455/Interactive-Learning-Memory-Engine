Minimum Radius of Gyration Principle

For single angle struts, structural buckling occurs about the axis with the least bending stiffness (least Moment of Inertia $I$). This is the principal v-v axis. It passes diagonally through the centroid, resulting in the absolute minimum radius of gyration, rmin. Designing against buckling about the x-x or y-y axes can lead to catastrophic design overestimates!
