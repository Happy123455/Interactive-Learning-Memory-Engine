Explain Like I'm 5 (ELI5)

Concrete is like a strong rock that loves to be squished (compression), but snaps easily if pulled. Steel bars are like super strong stretchy rubber bands that love to be pulled (tension). In an Under-Reinforced beam, the steel gives plenty of stretch warning before anything breaks!
