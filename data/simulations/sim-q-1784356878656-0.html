National Building Code (NBC) Orientation Norms

For hot-dry regions, orient the longest building facades facing North-South to block direct morning/evening radiant sun vectors. Westward walls must use structural shading devices.

Architectural Professional Ethics Code

An architect must design with public safety as paramount, maintain competitive transparency, and never accept secondary financial commissions or kickbacks from suppliers.
