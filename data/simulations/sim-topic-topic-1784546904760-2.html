Double-Ring Infiltrometer Physics

Standard infiltration instruments allow lateral water seepage, resulting in artificially high measurements. The double-ring system solves this. The outer ring acts as a hydraulic boundary buffer, forcing water in the inner ring to flow purely vertically. This ensures high-accuracy 1D infiltration measurement.

Primary Tasks:
  • Drag and Drop the infiltrometer across the soil zones (Sand, Silt, Clay) to see site variations.
  • Adjust variables (Rainfall, Infiltration rates) to observe runoff generation triggers.
  • Analyze the visual particle flows: Percolation is slow/deep, infiltration is fast/shallow, runoff is horizontal.
  • Listen closely: Simulated rainfall & runoff generate continuous custom synthesized audio feedback.
