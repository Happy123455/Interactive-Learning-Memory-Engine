Write an interactive, single-file HTML5/JS/CSS simulation explaining **four rigid pavement distresses** (Mud Pumping, Spalling, Corner Cracking, and Faulting) utilizing a **cyber-neon dashboard** combined with an **ELI5 Playful Sandbox pedagogy**. 

### UI & Layout (Strict 100vh, No Scrollbars)
* **Left Panel:** Cyberpunk glass container with 4 clickable Distress Cards, sliders for "Slab Stiffness" (Gummy Bear to Rock) and "Subgrade Wetness" (Dry Cookie to Soggy Pudding).
* **Right Panel/Canvas:** High-DPI 2D interactive canvas representing a pavement cross-section (Concrete Slab sitting on Soil Subgrade). 

### ELI5 & Gamified Interactive Mechanics
1. **Mouse Gesture/Swipe:** Users swipe their cursor across the slab. A "gummy bear" deformation mesh bends the slab dynamically under the mouse, displaying glowing pink/cyan stress vectors.
2. **Drag-and-Drop Elements:** Drag-and-drop "Heavy Toy Dinosaurs" (high axle load) or "Rain Clouds" directly onto the slab to trigger distresses.
3. **The 4 Distresses:**
   * **Mud Pumping ("Pudding Squirt"):** Dropping rain on a wet subgrade squirts bright green slurry up through joints when dinosaurs stomp.
   * **Corner Cracking ("Cracker Snap"):** Loading slab corners snaps them with a glowing fracture line and neon vector forces.
   * **Spalling ("Cookie Crumble"):** Dragging shear forces across joints shears off neon-green concrete flakes.
   * **Faulting ("Step-up Slide"):** Wet subgrade erosion drops one slab lower, creating a step-up bump.

### Sound, Pitch-Shifting Synth, & Narration
* **Web Audio Synth:** Link the "Slab Stiffness" and "Subgrade Wetness" sliders to an active oscillator. Lower stiffness shifts the tone to a deeper, warning rumble; higher water levels introduce a bubbling/gurgling filter effect.
* **Speech Synthesis:** Clicking any distress triggers `window.speechSynthesis` explaining the failure using childlike, enthusiastic food analogies (e.g., "Oh no! The water turned our soil into pudding, and now the heavy truck is squishing it right out of the crack!").
