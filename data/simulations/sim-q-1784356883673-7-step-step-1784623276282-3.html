Thermodynamic Principles

Indian passive architecture implements high thermal mass walls combined with dynamic courtyards and Jali screens. The Jali accelerates breeze through the Venturi effect while lowering air temp through evaporative moisture exchange. The central courtyard acts as a solar chimney, triggering stack effect during peak hot periods.

Conversely, the Western thermal envelope minimizes interaction with the exterior. It focuses on high-performance insulation, sealed airtight barriers, and controlled mechanical ventilation. High thermal mass here stabilizes indoor temperatures by delaying diurnal thermal transfer.
