INDIAN ROADS CONGRESS (IRC:90-2019) CODES

IRC:90 - SECTION 500: BITUMINOUS BASE AND BINDER COURSES

Governs the layout parameters of dynamic flexible asphalt roads. Paved bituminous concrete must achieve specified density parameters with zero mechanical void structures.

Layer Thickness ($h$)

Bituminous concrete base layer: Recommended standard minimum profile is exactly 40 mm thickness.

Standard Laydown Temp ($T$)

Compaction temperatures should stay between 120°C - 160°C. Target mixing and delivery temperature: 150 °C.

Roller Operating Speed ($v$)

Standard pneumatic/tandem steel rollers must move smoothly under 4 km/h to prevent dynamic displacement.

Required Density ($\rho$)

Field lab structural requirements specify minimum compaction densities: 98 % of theoretical maximum.

CRITICAL ENGINEERING RULE: Do not compact when asphalt cooling drops mix temperature below 120°C. Attempting compaction on cold mixes will fracture aggregate, leading to high voids and premature structural pothole creation!
