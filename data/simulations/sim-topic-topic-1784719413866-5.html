IS 456:2000 Codebook

Annex G Flanged Section Formulations

Clause 38.1
Assumptions in Limit State
The maximum compressive strain in concrete at the outermost compression fiber is taken as 0.0035 in bending. This produces a parabolic-rectangular stress block.
🔊 Narrate Clause
Annex G-2.2
Neutral Axis in Flange
When the neutral axis depth x_u is less than or equal to flange depth D_f, the section is computed as a rectangular beam of width equal to flange width b_f.
🔊 Narrate Clause
Annex G-2.3
Neutral Axis in Web (Modified Block)
When x_u > D_f, compression is calculated over the web and the outstanding flange. When flange is deep, a modified equivalent thickness y_f = 0.15·x_u + 0.65·D_f is used.
