Clause 26.5.1.1 - Minimum Tension Reinforcement
Minimum area of tension reinforcement shall not be less than: Ast / (b · d) = 0.85 / fy. This ensures a ductile failure state.
Clause 38.1 - Assumptions for Limit State of Collapse (Flexure)
Stress-strain behavior of concrete is parabolic-rectangular with design yield 0.446·fck. Equivalent compressive block results in total force: C = 0.36 · fck · b · xu.
Annex G.1.1 - Moment of Resistance Equations
Specifies the exact stress block center of gravity and equations for Mu: Mu = 0.87 · fy · Ast · d · [1 - (Ast · fy) / (b · d · fck)].
