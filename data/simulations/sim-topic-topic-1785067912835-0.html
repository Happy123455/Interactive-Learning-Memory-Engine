📖 Steel Buckling Lab Manual

Welcome to the cybernetic sandbox for Indian Standard IS 800:2007 cross-sectional classification. This lab models how local buckling influences steel member behavior.

Key Concepts & Physics:

  • Plastic (Class 1): Can develop full plastic hinge capacity and rotate without local buckling. Highly ductile.
  • Compact (Class 2): Reaches plastic stress states but cannot sustain rotation before local bucking.
  • Semi-Compact (Class 3): Extreme fiber reaches yield strength, but local buckling blocks full plastic capacity development.
  • Slender (Class 4): Local plate element buckles prematurely before reaching yielding limit.

Lab Actions:

1. Slide values on the left panel to dynamically change beam geometry.
2. Sweep/Drag directly on the central main canvas to apply load and see buckling deformations.
3. Complete the formula by dragging parameter chips b, t, and ε to the matching bottom tray slots.
