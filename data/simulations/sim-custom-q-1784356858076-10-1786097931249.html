Bitumen Ductility Test - Engineering Background

Standard Test Method: ASTM D113 / IS 1208

1. Objective & Definition

Ductility is defined as the distance in centimeters to which a standard briquette specimen of bitumen can be elongated without breaking when pulled apart at a specified speed and temperature.

2. Standard Test Parameters

  • Temperature: 25.0°C ± 0.5°C
  • Pulling Speed: 5.0 cm/min ± 5%
  • Initial Minimum Cross-Section (Waist): 10 mm × 10 mm (1.0 cm²)
  • Initial Clip Distance ($L_1$): 30 mm (3.0 cm)

3. Significance in Pavement Design

Bitumen must possess adequate ductility to prevent cracking under traffic loads and thermal expansion/contraction. A minimum ductility value of 50 cm to 100 cm is typically specified for paving grades in highway construction.

Briquette Brass End Clip

The briquette specimen is anchored by two precision-machined brass clips. As the pulling carriage moves, tensile force concentrates at these clip shoulders before distributing along the central bitumen neck.
