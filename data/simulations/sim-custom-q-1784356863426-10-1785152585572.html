IS 5851 / IRC RIGID PAVEMENT DESIGN EQUATIONS

1. Radius of Relative Stiffness ($l$)

$$l = \left( \frac{E \cdot h^3}{12 \cdot (1 - \nu^2) \cdot k} \right)^{1/4}$$

Measures relative stiffness of slab vs subgrade soil support. $E = 3 \times 10^5\ kg/cm^2$, Poisson's ratio $\nu = 0.15$.

2. Westergaard Load Stress Equations

Corner Load Stress ($\sigma_c$):
$$\sigma_c = \frac{3P}{h^2} \left[ 1 - \left( \frac{a\sqrt{2}}{l} \right)^{0.6} \right]$$
Edge Load Stress ($\sigma_e$):
$$\sigma_e = \frac{0.572 P}{h^2} \left[ 4 \log_{10}\left(\frac{l}{b}\right) + 0.359 \right]$$
Interior Load Stress ($\sigma_i$):
$$\sigma_i = \frac{0.316 P}{h^2} \left[ 4 \log_{10}\left(\frac{l}{b}\right) + 1.069 \right]$$

3. Bradbury's Thermal Curling Stress ($\sigma_t$)

$$\sigma_t = \frac{C \cdot E \cdot \alpha \cdot \Delta T}{2}$$

Where $C$ is Bradbury's curling coefficient ($C \approx 0.82$), $\alpha = 10 \times 10^{-6}\ /^\circ C$ thermal expansion of concrete.
