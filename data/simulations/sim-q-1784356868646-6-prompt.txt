Match a premium cyber-neon dark aesthetic (neon cyan, hot pink, lime) with bouncy toy-like physics, constrained within a strict 100vh layout to prevent double-scrolling.

### 1. Interactive Pavement Sandbox Canvas
* Draw a 4-layer dynamic flexible pavement cross-section (Seal Coat, Binder/Base, Subbase, Soil) with high-DPI canvas scaling.
* Support mouse/touch swipe gestures: sweeping across the surface bends the road like a gummy bear, showing real-time stress-strain vector overlays (tensile at bottom of asphalt, compressive in subgrade).
* Render real-time graphing of rut depth vs. repetition cycles and dynamic stress distribution curves.

### 2. Gamified Mechanics for the 4 Distresses
* **Rutting (Squeezed Playdough):** Drag heavy toy trucks over the road. Accumulate permanent depression in wheel paths. Show compression vector arrows squeezing the soil.

* **Bleeding (Melting Chocolate):** Slide a "Sun Heat" controller to make excess binder ooze to the surface with a glossy dark liquid texture.
* **Potholes (Pop-out Crumbs):** Drag water clouds to rain into cracks. Combine with truck tires to trigger pop-out structural physics, turning asphalt blocks into bouncing debris.

### 3. Drag-and-Drop & Audio Engine
* Drag concept labels (e.g., "Rutting", "Fatigue") and snap them onto active damage zones with green status rings and sound.
* **Synth:** Web Audio API oscillator linked to wheel pressure slider. Pitch-shifts dynamically (higher load = higher frequency squeak).
* **ELI5 Narration:** Native speech synthesis narrating distress events using fun analogies (e.g., "The hot sun is melting the road's glue!"). Include bouncy click/success chimes.
