IS 456:2000 Structural Code Clauses
Clause 25.1.1: Short vs. Slender Columns

A compression member is classified as short if both $L_e / D$ and $L_e / b$ are less than 12. If any ratio equals or exceeds 12, it is treated as a slender/long column.

Clause 25.4: Minimum Eccentricity

All columns must be designed for a minimum eccentricity of load equal to unsupported length of column divided by 500, plus lateral dimension divided by 30, subject to a minimum of 20 mm.

Euler's Buckling Theory Limit

Euler's theory assumes purely elastic buckling. When the slenderness ratio is small (below the transitional limit ~88.86 for standard mild steel), material yield dominates over geometric instability, rendering basic Euler equations non-conservative.
