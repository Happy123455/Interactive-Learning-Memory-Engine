💡 Quick Insight

WSM treats materials purely elastically. It models stresses linearly up to low working loads, resulting in thick, heavy sections. LSM models ultimate limit states, harnessing concrete's natural plastic zone, creating lighter, optimized, and safer structures.

🤖 Structural Sandbox Guide!

Welcome to the cyber-toybox structural physics engine! We will learn the difference between two critical philosophies:

1. Working Stress Method (WSM): Represented by Mr. Super-Stiff Hard Candy. He is conservative, behaves elastically, uses giant factor-of-safety margins, and refuses to bend. When pushed too far, he cracks catastrophically!

2. Limit State Method (LSM): Represented by Mr. Smart Gummy Bear. He is optimized, utilizes concrete's high performance plastic range, has balanced factors, and stretches safely under extreme tension before folding safely.

Your Mission: Drag the puzzle shields into their formula slots to unlock the sandbox engines, drop anxious elephants as weight vectors, and bend the structures directly!
