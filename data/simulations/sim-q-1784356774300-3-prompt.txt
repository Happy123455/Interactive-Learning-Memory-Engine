**Simulation Blueprint: "The Bolt-Builder Sandbox"**

**Objective**: Create a gamified, interactive structural simulation where users design bolted tension joints using ISA (Angle) sections and Gusset plates.

**Core Mechanics & UI**:
*   **Aesthetics**: Cyber-engineering dashboard with glassmorphism panels, dark mode, and neon-glowing accents (cyan for bolts, magenta for gussets).
*   **Layout**: Use a `CSS Grid` container with 100vh constraint. Left panel: Interactive Checklist & IS:800 Clause-Book. Center: 2D Canvas (High-DPI) for drag-and-drop structural assembly. Right panel: Real-time calculation graphs and audio controls.
*   **Drag-and-Drop**: Users drag "Bolt Cards" (M16-M36) and "Section Chips" onto the canvas. Snapping logic automatically aligns bolts within the GUSSET workspace with audible "chime" feedback on valid placement.

**ELi5 Playful Simulation Logic**:
*   **Analogy**: Explain tension joints as "Strong-Hands clasping a Tug-of-War rope." Use Web Speech Synthesis (`window.speechSynthesis`) to narrate: "Let’s keep the steel from snapping! Drag the bolts to pin these plates together."
*   **Motion**: Users sweep their mouse/hand across the canvas to "pull" the joint. As they pull, a wireframe diagram shows the "gummy-bear-like" stretch of the steel, with green-to-red color gradients indicating stress intensity.
*   **Audio Synthesis**: Connect a `Web Audio Oscillator`. Mapping joint-stress percentage to frequency (e.g., 200Hz base to 800Hz "warning whine" as capacity limits approach).

**Technical Requirements**:
*   **Canvas Animations**: Implement `requestAnimationFrame` for smooth growth of bolt holes and glowing rebar-heat effects (pulse animation) when a design parameter is successfully identified.
*   **Interactivity**: Clicking "Page 73" triggers a semi-transparent modal of IS:800-2007 tables. Dragging values from these tables into the active formula slots updates the total tension capacity in real-time.
*   **Visuals**: Show stress-strain diagrams that pivot in real-time as the user adjusts the pitch or gauge of the bolts. Use crisp, high-contrast vector lines for force vectors, dynamically resizing based on input.
