IS 800:2007 Design Code Reference

Indian Standard Code of Practice for General Construction In Steel

Clause 7.4.1 - Column Bases General General

Column bases shall be provided to distribute the load from the column over a sufficient area of concrete foundation so that the bearing pressure does not exceed permissible values.

Clause 7.4.3.1 - Slab Base Plate Thickness Key Formula

The minimum thickness of a rectangular slab base supporting axially loaded columns is calculated as:
t_s = √[ (2.5 w (a² - 0.3 b²) γ_m0) / f_y ]

Clause 7.4.3.2 - Gusseted Bases Stiffened

Gusseted bases are used for heavy loads or moments. Gusset plates and cleat angles stiffen the base plate, effectively reducing cantilever projections 'a' and required plate thickness!
