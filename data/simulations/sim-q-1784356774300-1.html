ELI5 Analogy Handbook

Black Bolts (Loose Pegs)

Imagine aligning two plates with a wooden peg in a slightly larger hole. They slip freely until the plate edges physically collide and lean against the peg. This relies entirely on bearing force.

HSFG Bolts (Magnetic Handshake)

Imagine two flat magnets sliding past each other. Massive clamping forces squeeze the plates. They won't budge even an inch until the pulling force completely overpowers the friction between them.
