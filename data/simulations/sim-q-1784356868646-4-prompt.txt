Write a single-file, highly interactive HTML5/JS/CSS simulation demonstrating "Highway Maintenance & Pavement Failures" under an **ELI5 Playful Sandbox** profile. 

### UI & Layout (100vh Viewport)
Build a premium dark dashboard with neon-glowing cyber-toy highlights (hot pink, electric cyan, bright lime). Use a 2-column flex/grid layout constrained to 100vh (no scrollbars).
* **Left Column:** A high-DPI HTML5 Canvas showing a 3-layer pavement cross-section (Subgrade, Base, Asphalt) with bouncing toy cars.
* **Right Column:** ELI5 control panels, real-time "Road Sickness" line graphs, and interactive "Tool Chest" cards.

### Core Gamified Mechanics
1. **Interactive Damage & Drag-and-Drop:** 
   * Trigger pavement failures: "Pothole Cavities", "Alligator Cracks" (like cracked glass), and "Rutting Marshmallows" (squeezed lanes) by adjusting **Traffic Weight** and **Rainfall** sliders.
   * Drag-and-drop **"Sticky Patch Band-Aids"**, **"Drainage Straws"**, or **"Stone Fillers"** directly onto damaged canvas hotspots to trigger animated healing glows, particle explosions, and score boosts.
2. **Gesture Interaction:** Sweep the cursor horizontally over water puddles to "sweep away" standing water, preventing subgrade softening.
3. **Advanced Visual Overlays:** Draw dynamic, real-time stress vectors (neon arrows showing load distribution) and color-coded moisture flow lines penetrating the road base.

### Audio & Speech Synthesis
* **Pitch-Shifting Synth:** Implement a continuous Web Audio API oscillator hum representing traffic rumble. Tie its frequency directly to the *Traffic Weight* and *Car Speed* sliders (higher weight = deeper growl; higher speed = higher pitch).
* **Sound Effects:** Synthesis-generated bouncy "boings", "splashes", and cheer sounds on successful pavement repairs.
* **ELI5 Speech Voiceover:** Use `window.speechSynthesis` to narrate playful pavement analogies when failures occur (e.g., *"Oh no! The water turned our road base into mushy pudding! Let's vacuum it out!"*).
