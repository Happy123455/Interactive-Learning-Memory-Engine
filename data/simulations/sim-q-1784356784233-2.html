Engineering Help & Definitions

Black Bolts (Ordinary Grade)

Transfers shear sideways across the interface when plates rub against each other. Subject to Shear Plane fracturing or Bearing (crushing) at the plate interfaces.

HSFG (High Strength Friction Grip)

Drawn tight to pre-tension structural components and clamp plates. Forces pass entirely through face-to-face friction rather than structural crushing.

Hole Clearance ($\Delta$)

Per Table 19 of IS 800:2007. Diameter $d_0 = d + 1\text{mm}$ (if $d \le 14$), $d + 2\text{mm}$ (if $16 \le d \le 24$).

End & Pitch Limits

Pitch spacing limit: Minimum $2.5 \times d$ distance to protect plates against local stresses. Edge spacing limit: Minimum $1.7 \times d_0$ (hand cut) or $1.5 \times d_0$ (machine flame-cut) to prevent shear block failure.
