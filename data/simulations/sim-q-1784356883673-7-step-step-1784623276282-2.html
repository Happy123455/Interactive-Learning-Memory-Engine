Stylistic Evolution: Theory Breakdown

In classical architectural theory, Indian architecture displays a spatial Syncretism—a structural mode where ancient traditional systems (Vastu-Purusha mandala geometry & trabeated load paths) seamlessly absorb external motifs.

Conversely, Western structures develop via Linear Succession, defined by dialectical transitions where incoming styles (e.g., Gothic) actively deconstruct and replace preceding paradigms (Romanesque).
