📚 Indian Roads Congress (IRC:73) Standard Guidelines

According to IRC:73 Standards, transition curves should be designed dynamically based on the following physical considerations:


  • Rate of Centrifugal Acceleration Change (C): Must stay between 0.5 and 0.8 m/s³ to safeguard passenger inner ears. Formula: C = 80 / (75 + V)
  • Minimum Length (Spiral): Must satisfy the rate of radial acceleration change: L = V³ / (C · R)
  • Spiral Form: The Euler spiral (clothoid) is standard as its curvature increases linearly with distance.
