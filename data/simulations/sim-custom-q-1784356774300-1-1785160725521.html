IS 800:2007 CODEBOOK EXCERPT

Bureau of Indian Standards - Design of Fasteners & Bolted Connections

Clause 10.3.2 - Shear Capacity of Bolt Page 75

The nominal shear capacity of a bolt V_nsb is calculated as:
V_nsb = (f_ub / √3) × (n_n · A_nb + n_s · A_sb)

Where f_ub is ultimate tensile strength of bolt, A_nb is net stress area at threads (0.78 × π/4 × d²), and n_n is number of shear planes passing threads.

Clause 10.3.3 - Bearing Capacity of Plate Page 75

The nominal bearing strength of bolt on plate V_npb is given by:
V_npb = 2.5 · k_b · d · t · f_u

Where k_b depends on edge distance e, pitch distance p, and bolt/plate ultimate strength ratio.

Clause 10.4.3 - High Strength Friction Grip (HSFG) Bolts Page 76

Nominal slip resistance is provided purely by friction induced by bolt clamping tension:
V_nsf = μ_f · n_f · K_h · F_o

Where F_o = 0.7 · f_ub · A_nb is minimum bolt proof tension, and μ_f is slip factor coefficient of interface.
