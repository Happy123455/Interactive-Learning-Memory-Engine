IRC & IS-CODE REFERENCE DESK

Drag specification clauses out of this handbook into your workplace layout to unlock advanced computation and construction algorithms.

IRC:19-2005 (Water Bound Macadam Specification)
Governs use of coarse aggregate, screenings, and binding material. Prescribes dry rolling first, followed by screenings application, wet rolling, and dynamic void filling. Maximum loose layer thickness 100mm.
IRC:109-2015 (Wet Mix Macadam Specification)
Prescribes graded aggregates ranging from 4.75mm to 40mm blended with targeted moisture (OMC: 5-7%) in a mixing plant before laying. Minimum thickness 75mm. Rapid compaction prevents dry segregation.
Section 401: MoRT&H (Subgrade Specifications)
Requires compacting subgrade layer to 98% relative compaction using heavy vibratory rollers to maintain a load bearing base for Macadam configurations.
