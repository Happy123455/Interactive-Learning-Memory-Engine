1.2 LIMITING DEPTH OF NEUTRAL AXIS
For Fe 415, maximum ratio xu,max / d = 0.48
Limiting Depth xu,max = 0.48 × d
xu,max = 216.0 mm
1.3 LIMITING MOMENT OF RESISTANCE (Mu,lim)
Mu,lim = 0.36 · fck · b · xu,max · (d - 0.42 · xu,max)
Mu,lim = 0.36 · 25 · 300 · 216 · (450 - 0.42 · 216)
Mu,lim = 130.6 × 106 N-mm
Mu,lim = 130.60 kNm

Doubly Reinforced Section Required
Since the external design bending moment exceeds the maximum capacity of a singly reinforced balanced section, compression reinforcement must be added in the compression zone.
