IS 800 : 2007 - Slab Base Standard

Bureau of Indian Standards Reference

Clause 7.4.3.1: In design of column bases, the slab base thickness (ts) must verify bearing and flexural stresses.

The calculation assumes an elastic stress distribution and partial safety factor for material strength (γm0) of 1.10.

Ensure the concrete pedestal grade offers a bearing capacity matching at least 0.45 x fck to avoid compression failure under extreme loads.
