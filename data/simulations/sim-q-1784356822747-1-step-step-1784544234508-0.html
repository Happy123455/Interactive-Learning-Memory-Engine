Step 1: Trial Dimensioning & Limiting Constants

1.1 Effective Depth Formulation

The trial section is modeled using concrete overall depth ($D$) and nominal cover ($c$).

d = D - c d = 500 - 50 = 450 mm

1.2 Limiting Depth of Neutral Axis (x_u,max)

For design steel grade, the limiting ratio of depth of neutral axis is governed by IS-456 Clause 38.1:

xu,max / d = 0.48 (for Fe415) xu,max = 0.48 × 450 = 216 mm

1.3 Limiting Moment Capacity (M_u,lim)

Maximum moment capacity without compression reinforcement:

Mu,lim = 0.138 · fck · b · d² Mu,lim = 0.138 · 20 · 300 · 450² × 10⁻⁶ = 167.67 kNm
