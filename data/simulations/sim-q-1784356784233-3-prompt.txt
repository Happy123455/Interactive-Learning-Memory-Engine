To ensure absolute structural accuracy, write the core calculations in JavaScript using these exact values based on `ISA 100 x 100 x 10 mm` (with standard assumptions where needed, but fully dynamic):

- **Net Area of Connected Leg ($A_{nc}$)**:

  $$A_{go} = \left(b_2 - \frac{t}{2}\right) \cdot t$$

  $$b_s = b_2 + g - t$$
- **Length of Connection ($L_c$)**:
  $$L_c = (n - 1) \cdot p$$
