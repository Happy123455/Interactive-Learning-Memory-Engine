Historical Context

The Trabeated system (seen in Ajanta cave frames and early Dravidian temples) utilizes monumental post-and-beam configurations. Maximum span length is heavily constrained by stone tensile strength, resulting in massive material volume.

Structural Efficiency

Bending forces require thick stone beams to counter dangerous tensile stresses. In comparison, Arcuate arches redirect massive dead load entirely into compression vectors pushing outwards against the support.
