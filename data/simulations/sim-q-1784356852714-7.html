Concept Masterclass: Overtaking Sight Distance

Overtaking Sight Distance (OSD) represents the minimum open roadway length visible to a driver required to safely complete an overtaking maneuver without risking collision with potential oncoming traffic traveling at design speeds.

Critical Phasing Components:
  • d1 (Reaction Segment): Distance traveled by overtaking vehicle during the initial perception-reaction period.
  • d2 (Overtaking Action): Distance covered while completing path shifts around the slower target vehicle.
  • d3 (Oncoming Hazard Segment): Safe distance buffer traversed by incoming oncoming vehicles during transaction phases.

IRC recommendations assume conservative spacing calculations and require precise alignment parameters for two-lane, standard continuous roadways. Dynamic sliders calibrate this cyber-physics model instantly.

MATH FORMULA OSD = d1 + d2 + d3
