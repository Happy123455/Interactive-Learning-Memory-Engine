Simulation Instructions

👆
**Drag & Drop:** Pick up soil cards and numerical chips from the control panel and drop them into designated zones on the canvas or control panel.
🎚️
**Sliders:** Adjust values like 'Compaction Level' using the sliders in the control panel.
↔️
**Hand Gesture (Canvas):** During the test, hold down your mouse button and sweep left/right on the canvas to control the plunger's penetration speed.
📐
**Tangent Ruler (Canvas):** When a concave curve appears, drag the glowing ruler on the canvas to align it as a tangent to the steepest part of the curve.
📖
**Codebook:** Click the 'IS 456 Codebook' to reveal important rules for CBR selection.
