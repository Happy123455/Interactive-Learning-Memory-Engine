Advantages of Modification

High Temperature Permanent Deformation (Rutting)

Polymer modification (e.g., SBS elastomers) creates a continuous 3D polymer crosslinked matrix inside the asphalt binder. Under heavy slow-moving truck axle loads at high summer pavement temperatures (60°C+), the modified binder recovers elastically, preventing permanent wheel channel rut depth.
