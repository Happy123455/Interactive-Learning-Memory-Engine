STEP 2.1: LIMIT STATE EVALUATION IS 456 Annex G

Design Moment, Mu = 280.0 kNm

Concrete Grade: M20, Steel Grade: Fe 415

b = 300 mm | d = 450 mm

1. Calculating Limiting Capacity (Mu,lim):

Mu,lim = K × fck × b × d2

Since Steel is Fe 415, limiting parameter K = 0.138

Mu,lim = 0.138 × 20 × 300 × 4502 × 10-6

Mu,lim = 167.67 kNm

2. Capacity Comparison:

Mu (280.0 kNm) > Mu,lim (167.67 kNm)

Conclusion: Doubly Reinforced Section is Required

3. Calculation of Excess Moment (Mu2):

Mu2 = Mu - Mu,lim

Mu2 = 280.0 - 167.67

Mu2 = 112.33 kNm

This remaining balance of 112.33 kNm will be resisted by the addition of compression reinforcement (Asc) and balancing tension reinforcement (Ast2).
