Load Distribution Mechanism

Flexible pavements distribute concentrated wheel loads layer-by-layer through grain-to-grain contact, reducing vertical stress down to the subgrade. Rigid pavements act as flexural beams, spanning soft subgrades using slab flexural rigidity ($E_c$).

150-Micron Separation Membrane

Placed between the PQC slab and DLC sub-base in rigid pavements. Prevents bonding to reduce frictional restraint during thermal contraction, avoids cement slurry loss during PQC placement, and acts as a moisture barrier.

Subgrade Preparation Norms

Compacted subgrade foundation must be minimum 500 mm thick for Expressways/Highways and minimum 300 mm for Rural Roads (IRC:37/IRC:SP:20). Requires minimum 97% MDD compaction and target CBR ≥ 8%.
