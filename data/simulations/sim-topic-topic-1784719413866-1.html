Limit State Equilibrium Studio

Welcome to the high-fidelity concrete analysis simulator. Master the calculation of neutral axis depth and structural failure classification through five tactile steps:

  • Data Extraction: Click highlighted variables inside the Left Design Brief to feed the physical specs into your CAD workstation.
  • IS Code Verification: Drag the IS 456 Annexure G card into the Assembly Desk workspace to retrieve the balance formulas.
  • Formula Assembly: Drag-and-drop structural chips into correct slots to compute the theoretical neutral axis ($x_{u} \approx 121$ mm).
  • Interactive Calibration: Utilize the real-time slider to override and manipulate the depth of the concrete compression block.
  • Equilibrium Analysis: Examine how the Compressive Force ($C$) balances against Tensile Capacity ($T$) and witness ductile vs. brittle failure mechanisms.
