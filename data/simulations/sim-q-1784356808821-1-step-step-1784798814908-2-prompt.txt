You are an elite educational software developer. Your task is to architect a high-fidelity, interactive **Structural Engineering Simulation** for the "Iterative Calculation of Actual Neutral Axis Depth ($x_u$)" in Doubly Reinforced RCC Beams.

### 🎯 Objective
Create a gamified, browser-based studio that forces the user to solve the iterative equilibrium balance ($C_u = T_u$) for a beam with tension and compression reinforcement.

### 🏗️ Technical Specifications & UI
1. **Studio Environment**: Use a dark mode aesthetic (`#0f172a` background) with glassmorphism panels. The canvas must feature a **Dynamic Section Draft** of a beam (width $b$, depth $D$, $A_{st}$, $A_{sc}$) that updates its wireframe in real-time as parameters are adjusted.
2. **Interactive Controls**:
   - **Variable Highlight**: The problem statement is in the sidebar; clicking a variable (e.g., "$f_{sc}$") highlights its corresponding region on the canvas beam section.
   - **Formula Drop Slots**: A central workspace with "Target Slots." Users must drag numerical cards or variable chips (e.g., $0.36 \cdot f_{ck} \cdot b \cdot x_u$) into these slots to assemble the equation.
   - **Iteration Slider**: A dedicated "Iteration Controller" with a scrubbable $x_u$ value. As the user slides $x_u$, the $C_u$ and $T_u$ bars in a stacked chart animate toward equilibrium.
3. **Engineering Mechanics**:
   - **Live Stress-Strain Pivot**: A secondary side-panel showing the concrete parabolic stress block and linear strain diagram. As $x_u$ changes, the neutral axis depth line on the strain diagram must physically move up/down, causing the strain in compression steel ($\epsilon_{sc}$) to update dynamically.
   - **Camera Zooms**: Trigger a 150% zoom on the Compression Steel zone when the user engages the iterative lookup for $f_{sc}$ (referencing the SP-16/IS 456 curve).
4. **Gamification & Feedback**:
   - **Soundscapes**: Play low-frequency "thrum" clicks for UI interactions, a success "chime" when equations balance, and a "warning" buzz if the user hits an unrealistic $x_u$ depth.
   - **Voice Narration**: Use `window.speechSynthesis` to provide brief, professional academic guidance. *Example: "The strain in compression steel depends on the neutral axis depth. Slide the axis until compression force matches tension force."*
