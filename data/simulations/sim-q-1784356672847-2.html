IS 456 Codebook Insights

Concrete Cover (Cl. 26.4)

Minimum clear cover required for durability and fire resistance. Varies based on exposure conditions (mild, moderate, severe, etc.) and bar diameter. Usually 20mm to 50mm for beams.

Shear Reinforcement (Cl. 26.5.1)

Stirrups (or links) resist shear forces. Spacing and diameter are critical. Minimum shear reinforcement required even if not needed by calculation.

Longitudinal Reinforcement (Cl. 26.5.3)

Minimum and maximum areas of tension and compression steel. Spacing of bars to ensure proper concrete flow and bond. Bar curtailment rules.

Development Length (Cl. 26.2)

The length of embedment required to develop the full design strength of a bar. Critical at beam-column junctions for proper load transfer (e.g., dowels).

Binding Wire Practices

While not explicitly in IS 456 for calculation, proper tying (binding) of bars is standard practice to maintain cage integrity during concrete pouring and vibration.
