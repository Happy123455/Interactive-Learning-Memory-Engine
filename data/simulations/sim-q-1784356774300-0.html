IS 800 Engineering Specification Manual

Official Standards Guideline for Joint Design & Bolt Spacings

Clause 10.2.2: Minimum Pitch Distance

The distance between center of fasteners in the direction of load shall not be less than 2.5 times the nominal diameter of the fastener. Spacings too close can yield high stress concentrations causing tear-out.

Clause 10.2.4: Minimum Edge & End Distance

The distance from the center of any fastener to the nearest edge of a plate shall not be less than 1.5 times the hole diameter for machine flame-cut edges, and 1.7 times for hand-cut sheared edges. This prevents edge bearing failure.
