IS 800:2007 CODEBOOK CLAUSES REFERENCE

Clause 10.3.3 — Shear Capacity of Bolt ($V_{dsb}$) Page 75

The nominal shear capacity of a bolt $V_{nsb}$ is given by:

$V_{nsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot (n_n \cdot A_{nb} + n_s \cdot A_{sb})$

Design shear strength: $V_{dsb} = \frac{V_{nsb}}{\gamma_{mb}}$ where $\gamma_{mb} = 1.25$.

Active Live Calculation: $V_{dsb} = \frac{400}{\sqrt{3} \times 1.25} \times 198.56 = \mathbf{36.68\text{ kN}}$
Clause 10.3.4 — Bearing Capacity of Bolt ($V_{dpb}$) Page 75

The design bearing strength of bolt $V_{dpb} = \frac{V_{npb}}{\gamma_{mb}}$, where:

$V_{npb} = 2.5 \cdot k_b \cdot d \cdot t \cdot f_u$

where $k_b = \min\left( \frac{e}{3 d_0}, \frac{p}{3 d_0} - 0.25, \frac{f_{ub}}{f_u}, 1.0 \right)$.

Active Live Calculation: $V_{dpb} = \frac{2.5 \times 0.50 \times 18 \times 8 \times 410}{1.25} = \mathbf{58.96\text{ kN}}$
Clause 6.3.1 — Tensile Strength due to Rupture ($T_{dn}$) Page 32
$T_{dn} = \frac{0.9 \cdot A_n \cdot f_u}{\gamma_{m1}}$

$A_n$ is the net cross-sectional area after deducting bolt holes: $(p - d_0) \cdot t$.
