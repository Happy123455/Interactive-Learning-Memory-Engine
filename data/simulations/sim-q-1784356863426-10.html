Essential Guidelines

Corner vs. Edge Loading

Under thermal gradients, slab warps. Day-time edge load & night-time corner load are historically calculated as extreme states.

Standard Subgrade reaction

Measured using a 75 cm standard plate load test. Minimum desired Modulus value for national highway design is 40 MPa/m.
