Build an interactive, gamified "Bolted Connection Sandbox" using HTML5 Canvas, GSAP for fluid animations, and Web Audio API. 

**Visual & UX Architecture:**
- **Theme:** Cyber-engineering dark aesthetic. Use translucent glass-morphism panels, glowing neon (cyan/purple) highlights, and 100vh containment. 
- **Canvas:** Implement a high-DPI responsive grid. Include an "ELI5" toggle to switch between formal structural engineering views and playful, bouncy 2D vector diagrams.

**Core Interactions:**
- **The "Bolt Lab" Playground:** Provide a drag-and-drop workspace where users pull "Bolt Types" (Black, Turned, HSFG) onto steel plate overlaps (Lap/Butt joints).
- **Physical Feedback:** Dropping a bolt into a hole triggers a "clink" sound (Web Audio oscillator). Dragging a "Load Slider" creates real-time force vectors (arrows) on the bolts, with color-shifting from green (safe) to pulsing red (failure/slip).
- **Gesture Control:** Allow mouse-sweep gestures across the joint to simulate physical sliding; use pitch-shifting oscillators (higher frequency = higher tension) to provide auditory feedback on structural stress.
- **Dynamic Formula Slots:** Use floating "Property Chips" (e.g., M16, Grade 8.8). Users must drag these chips into formula drop-zones to unlock calculation outputs. Snap-to-target animations with success chimes are required.

**Pedagogy & Narration:**
- **Voiceover:** Use `window.speechSynthesis` to provide friendly, simplified narrations (e.g., "Oh no! Too much weight makes the connection slip! Try a stronger HSFG bolt instead.").
- **ELI5 Analogies:** Compare "Slip" to a stack of wet slides, and "HSFG" to a super-strong magnetic handshake.
- **IS 800 References:** Include a floating, interactive "Codebook" widget. Clicking it allows users to flip through Page 73/76. When the user identifies a clause, highlight the corresponding active formula component on the canvas in glowing neon.

**Technical Constraints:**
- No static text boxes; all info must be part of the interactive, animated UI. 
- Ensure smooth 60fps interaction; use `requestAnimationFrame` for all movement.
