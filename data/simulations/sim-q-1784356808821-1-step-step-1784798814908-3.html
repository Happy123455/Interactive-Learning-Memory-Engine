Step 4 Mechanics & IS Code Derivation Rules

Compression Stress Block

The concrete stress profile is defined as parabolic up to a strain of 0.002, and rectangular up to a peak value of 0.0035. The centroid of the concrete compressive force lies at a distance of \(0.42 x_u\) from the topmost compression fiber, yielding an average stress factor of \(0.36 f_{ck}\).

Limiting Neutral Axis Depth (\(x_{u,max}\))

Ensures tension steel yields before concrete crushing, safeguarding ductile failure. Computed from the linear strain profile when concrete outermost strain reaches exactly \(0.0035\) and tension steel reaches its exact threshold yield strain of \(\frac{0.87 f_y}{E_s} + 0.002\).

Failure Dynamics

When \(x_u < x_{u,max}\), steel yields first (Ductile). If \(x_u > x_{u,max}\), concrete reaches strain threshold of \(0.0035\) before steel yields. This leads to explosive, brittle failure without warning, strictly prohibited by IS 456 for design unless transformed into a doubly reinforced section.
