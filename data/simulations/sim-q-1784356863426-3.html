IRC:37-2018 Codebook Reference

The Vehicle Damage Factor (VDF) is a multiplier to convert number of commercial vehicles of different axle loads and configurations to the number of standard axle load repetitions (80 kN).

Clause 3.1.2: Equivalency Factor Formula
For Single Axle Load (L) in kN:
Equivalency Factor = ( L / 80 )^4
Standard Axle Load (L_s) = 80 kN (Single) / 100 kN (Tandem)
