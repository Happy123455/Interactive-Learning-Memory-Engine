Indian Standard (IS 800:2007) Steel Code

Section 7: Column Bases & Pedestals

For structural stability of members transmitting heavy concentric load components from structural columns to foundations, bases must spread structural load distributions evenly.

Clause 7.4.3 Slab Base Plate Thickness Verification

The minimum plate thickness, ts, for a structural column base transmitting axial load, is calculated as follows:

ts = √[ 2.5 × w × (a² - 0.3b²) × γm0 / fy ]

Where:
• w: Actual bearing pressure under structural plate (N/mm²).
• a, b: Overhang projections beyond column flange edges (mm).
• fy: Yield strength of base plate material (N/mm²).
• γm0: Partial safety factor (1.1).
