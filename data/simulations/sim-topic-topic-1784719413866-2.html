Annex G.1.1 (Moment of Resistance Formula)
For under-reinforced sections, the limiting/ultimate value of moment of resistance ($M_u$) is calculated using: $$M_u = 0.87 f_y A_{st} d \left(1 - \frac{A_{st} f_y}{b d f_{ck}}\right)$$ *Drag this entire Annex box to step 1 match-zone to confirm structural validity.*
