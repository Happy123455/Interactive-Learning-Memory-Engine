IS 800:2007 CLAUSE REFERENCE

Design of Fasteners & Bolt Connections

CLAUSE 10.2.2 & 10.2.4 Pitch & Edge Limits

Minimum Pitch ($p_{min}$): Shall not be less than $2.5 \times d$ (nominal bolt diameter).
Minimum Edge Distance ($e_{min}$): Shall not be less than $1.5 \times d_0$ for machine cut edges, or $1.7 \times d_0$ for hand cut edges.

CLAUSE 10.3.3 Shear Capacity ($V_{dsb}$)
$$V_{nsb} = \frac{f_u}{\sqrt{3}} \left( n_n A_{nb} + n_s A_{sb} \right)$$ $$V_{dsb} = \frac{V_{nsb}}{\gamma_{mb}} \quad (\gamma_{mb} = 1.25)$$
CLAUSE 10.3.4 Bearing Capacity ($V_{dpb}$)
$$V_{npb} = 2.5 \cdot k_b \cdot d \cdot t \cdot f_u$$ $$k_b = \min\left( \frac{e}{3 d_0}, \frac{p}{3 d_0} - 0.25, \frac{f_{ub}}{f_u}, 1.0 \right)$$
CLAUSE 10.4.3 HSFG Slip Resistance ($V_{dsf}$)
$$V_{dsf} = \frac{\mu_f \cdot n_f \cdot K_h \cdot F_o}{\gamma_{mf}} \quad (F_o = 0.7 f_{ub} A_{nb})$$
