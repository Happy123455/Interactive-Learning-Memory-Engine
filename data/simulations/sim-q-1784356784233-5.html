📖 IS 800:2007 ENGINEERING CODEX

1. Tensile Capacity (T_d) Modes:

According to Indian Standard IS 800:2007 Clause 6.1, the design strength of a tension member is the minimum of:

  • Yielding of Gross Section (Cl 6.2):
    T_dg = A_g × f_y / γ_m0 (Prevents huge mechanical deformations).
  • Rupture of Net Section (Cl 6.3.3):
    For single-angle connected sections: T_dn = 0.9 × A_nc × f_u / γ_m2 + β × A_go × f_y / γ_m0
    Where β represents the shear-lag reduction factor due to tension load non-uniformity across outstanding flange.
  • Block Shear Strength (Cl 6.4):
    Shear plane fails while tensile plane ruptures. It's evaluated as the minimum of formulas T_db1 and T_db2.

2. Bolt Limits (Clause 10.2):

  • Min Pitch (p): 2.5 × d (To protect steel connection elements from bearing failure).
  • Min Edge (e): 1.5 × d₀ (For hand-cut holes) or 1.7 × d₀.
