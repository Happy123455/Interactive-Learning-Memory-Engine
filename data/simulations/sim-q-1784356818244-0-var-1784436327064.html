Interactive Visual Dictionary

Click cards to highlight where this physical factor is acting inside the beam.

Ld : Development Length Req. Target

The minimum length of rebar that must be embedded in concrete to fully transmit the steel's yield strength to concrete via bond stress. If embedment length is shorter, the bar will pull out!

Le : Embedded/Available Length Physical

The actual, physical length of the steel bar placed inside the concrete support. For structural safety, we must always ensure that Le ≥ Ld.

φ : Bar Diameter Rebar Thickness

The thickness of the rebar. Thicker bars possess higher cross-sectional areas, carrying immense forces. Consequently, they require deeper embedment lengths to transmit those larger loads safely.

τbd : Design Bond Stress Grip Adhesive Capacity

The shear stress resisting slippage at the steel-concrete boundary. It acts like structural glue. It is increased by higher concrete grades and mechanical rib deformations.

fy : Steel Yield Strength Grade Limit

The maximum design load stress the steel reinforcing bar is allowed to handle before permanently deforming. Higher grades need more anchor depth to develop full tensile yield.
