IS 456 Limit State Design Tutorial

1. Singly Reinforced RCC Beams: The cross-section consists of concrete resisting compressive stresses in the top compression zone, and steel reinforcing bars resisting tensile forces in the bottom tension zone.

2. Strain Compatibility: Standard codes specify that the maximum design compressive strain at the outermost concrete fiber is restricted to exactly 0.0035 in bending.

3. Concrete Stress Block: The compressive stress distribution curves from zero at the Neutral Axis (N.A.) as a parabola up to a strain of 0.002, and then remains rectangularly constant at 0.446 fck up to a strain of 0.0035.

4. Under-Reinforced Sections: If xu < xu,max, the steel yields before the concrete crushes, resulting in safe, ductile failures with large visual warning signs.

5. Over-Reinforced Sections: If xu > xu,max, concrete undergoes brittle compression failure with no visual warning. IS 456 limits moment calculations of such sections to the balanced condition limits.
