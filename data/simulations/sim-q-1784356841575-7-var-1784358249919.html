What is the W-index?

The W-index is the average rate of infiltration during the entire storm duration. It assumes losses are uniformly spread. Formula:

W = (P - R) / T

Where P = Total rainfall, R = Runoff, T = Duration of storm.

What is the Φ-index?

The Φ-index is the infiltration rate above which rainfall volume equals direct runoff. Any rainfall occurring at an intensity below the Φ-index only fills up soil voids (infiltration) and produces zero surface runoff.

Φ = (P_e - R) / T_e

Where P_e = Effective precipitation (excluding hours with intensity ≤ Φ), and T_e = Effective duration of excess storm.

Hydrologic Conservation: The physical core of these methods ensures that the volume of rainfall falling *above* the horizontal Φ-index threshold perfectly equals the total Direct Runoff measured in the stream.
