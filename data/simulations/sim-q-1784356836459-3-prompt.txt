### 2. Physical Engine Formulas (IS-456 Alignment):
* **Ultimate Moment Capacity ($M_{u, \lim}$)**:
  Compute Neutral Axis depth factor $x_{u, \max} / d = 0.48$ (for Fe 415).
  $$M_{u,\lim} = 0.36 \cdot f_{ck} \cdot b \cdot x_{u,\max} \cdot (d - 0.42 \cdot x_{u,\max})$$
* **Cracking Moment ($M_{cr}$)**:
  Calculate flexural tensile strength $f_{cr} = 0.7 \cdot \sqrt{f_{ck}}$.
  Calculate gross moment of inertia $I_g = \frac{b \cdot D^3}{12}$.
  $$M_{cr} = \frac{f_{cr} \cdot I_g}{(D/2)}$$
* **Live Deflection ($\delta$)**:
  Compute live loading moment $M_{max} = \frac{(w_{applied} + w_{hand}) \cdot L^2}{8}$.
  Compute deflection using dynamic effective moment of inertia $I_{eff}$:
  If $M_{max} < M_{cr}$, use $I_g$. If $M_{max} \ge M_{cr}$, interpolate cracked section inertia $I_{cr}$:
  $$I_{cr} = \frac{b \cdot x_{u}^3}{3} + \left(\frac{E_s}{E_c}\right) \cdot A_{st} \cdot (d - x_{u})^2$$
  where $x_u$ is solved from $\frac{b \cdot x_u^2}{2} = \left(\frac{E_s}{E_c}\right) \cdot A_{st} \cdot (d - x_u)$.
  $$\delta = \frac{5 \cdot w \cdot L^4}{384 \cdot E_c \cdot I_{eff}}$$
  *Allowable Serviceability Limit*: $\delta_{\max} = \frac{L}{250}$.

function initAudio() {
  audioCtx = new (window.AudioContext || window.webkitAudioContext)();
  beamOscillator = audioCtx.createOscillator();

  beamOscillator.type = 'sawtooth';

  filter.connect(structuralGain);
  structuralGain.connect(audioCtx.destination);

  beamOscillator.start();
}
