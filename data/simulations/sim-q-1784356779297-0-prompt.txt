**Simulation Blueprint: The "Steel-Joint Playground"**

**Objective**: Build a high-fidelity interactive simulation for "Welded Connections vs. Bolted Connections" using an ELI5 pedagogical framework.

**1. Core Architecture**:
*   **Viewport**: 100vh CSS Grid, dark glass-morphism aesthetic (neon cyan/purple accents). No sidebar text; all instructional content must be dynamic tooltips or canvas callouts.
*   **Canvas Interaction**: High-DPI HTML5 canvas featuring two side-by-side structural joints: a **Welded Connection** (molten glowing aesthetic) and a **Bolted Connection** (mechanical toy aesthetic).

**2. Pedagogical Mechanics (ELI5 Playful Sandbox)**:
*   **The "Glue vs. Bolt" Analogy**: Use "Magic Glowy Glue" (Welding) vs. "Clicky Toy Fasteners" (Bolting) narration.
*   **Gestural Control**: Users sweep their mouse across the canvas to apply "Wiggle Stress." Upon sweeping, the Welded joint remains rigid (pulsing glow), while the Bolted joint shows minor slippage and vibration.
*   **Audio Feedback**: Integrate Web Audio API. Moving the slider or mouse creates a pitch-shifted "squeak" (higher stress = higher pitch). Use `speechSynthesis` to narrate: "Look! Welding acts like a permanent fuse, while bolts are like building blocks we can take apart!"

**3. Interactive Elements**:
*   **Drag-and-Drop Formula Slots**: Create floating "Value Cards" (e.g., *Noise Level*, *Strength*, *Reusability*). Users drag these onto the "Welded" or "Bolted" zones.
*   **Feedback Loops**: Successful drops trigger a "Success Chime" and unlock a 3D overlay of force vectors. Incorrect drops trigger a "Boing" sound and a playful screen shake.
*   **Structural Zoom**: Use dynamic `context.scale()` to zoom into the welding bead or bolt shank when clicking, showing real-time stress-strain diagrams.

**4. Visual Specs**:
*   **Welding Animation**: Use `requestAnimationFrame` for a "pulsing liquid" effect on weld lines.
*   **Bolted Animation**: Sprites for bolts that "wiggle" during stress tests.
*   **Constraint**: All UI must be responsive. Use `window.onresize` to scale the coordinate system, ensuring perfect alignment of force vectors and structural profiles.
