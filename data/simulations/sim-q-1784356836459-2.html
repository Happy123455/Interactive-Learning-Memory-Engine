🛡️ Mission Briefing: The Safety Shield Playground!

Welcome future engineer! Our structures must stand the test of time and chaos. This playground challenges you to configure structural components to withstand heavy loads using the Limit State Method (IS 456 / IS 800).

Your Tasks:
1. Click & drag directly on the structural horizontal beam to apply dynamic hand-pulled loads!
2. Toggle between Gummy Concrete & Licorice Steel.
3. Correctly drag & drop circular Material Factors (γm) and Load Factors (γf) into their equation slots.
4. Turn on the Synthesizer to listen to safety levels as audio wave frequencies!

💡 Hint: If the loads are factored incorrectly (using factor 1.00), the beam will experience structural collapse! Drag shields to protect the structure!
