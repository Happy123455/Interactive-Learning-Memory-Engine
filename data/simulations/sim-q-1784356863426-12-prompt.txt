### PROMPT FOR GEMINI: ADVANCED INTERACTIVE SIMULATION ARCHITECT

**Role**: You are a Lead Frontend Engineer and Educational Game Designer specializing in high-fidelity Engineering Simulations.

**Objective**: Build a single-file interactive simulation (HTML5/CSS3/Vanilla JS) demonstrating the **Construction Process of Concrete Pavement (Transportation Engineering)**. The goal is to move beyond static text and create a "Construction Studio" where the user physically manages the pavement layering process.

---

### 1. CORE ARCHITECTURE & UI/UX
*   **Layout**: Use a rigid CSS Grid/Flex layout (`100vh`) with a dark, cyberpunk engineering aesthetic: translucent glass panels, neon borders (cyan for active states, purple for inert), and a unified dashboard.
*   **The Canvas**: A high-DPI responsive `<canvas>` that serves as the central construction zone. Use `requestAnimationFrame` for fluid movement.
*   **Viewport**: Strict containment—no overflow. Everything must scale dynamically to the browser window.
*   **Dashboard**: Sidebar with a "Construction Queue" and a "Materials Depot."

### 2. GAMIFIED SIMULATION MECHANICS
*   **Construction Steps (Drag-and-Drop)**: Create a "Materials Depot" containing icon-cards: *Subgrade, Sub-base, Concrete Mix, Vibrator, Texturing Tool, Curing Compound*. 
*   **Interactive Interaction**: 
    *   The user must drag these cards into a "Construction Slot" in the center canvas.
    *   **Snap-to-Layer**: When a layer (e.g., Concrete Mix) is dropped onto the sub-base, trigger a "Pour Animation" (particle system) and a "Leveling" transition.
    *   **Real-time Feedback**: As each layer is placed, a "Quality Inspector" panel (a neon HUD) updates with progress and engineering metrics (e.g., compaction density, water-cement ratio, thickness).
*   **Vector/Velocity Overlays**: When the user drags a "Vibrator" tool, render a circular "force field" overlay on the canvas that pulsates to show compaction range.

### 3. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
*   **Audio/Voice**:
    *   **Sound**: Use Web Audio API (Oscillators) to generate a "Chime" on successful drop, a "Thud" for heavy materials, and a subtle "Buzz" for the vibrator tool.
    *   **Narration**: Integrate `window.speechSynthesis` to provide contextual explanations when a tool is dropped (e.g., "Compaction initiated: Ensuring subgrade density reaches 98%").
*   **Dynamic Equation Graphing**: Display a "Quality vs. Time" graph in the footer. As layers are placed, use Chart.js or a custom Canvas path drawing to plot the "Pavement Life-cycle" growth in real-time.

### 4. SIMULATION STEPS (THE "CONSTRUCTION FLOW")
1.  **Step 1: Prep Subgrade**: Drag a "Roller" card. Animate the surface becoming flat/compact.
2.  **Step 2: Sub-base Layer**: Drag "Aggregates" into the canvas. Show particle distribution.
3.  **Step 3: Concrete Pour**: Drag the "Concrete" chip. Trigger a fill animation, showing the transition from liquid to solid.
4.  **Step 4: Finishing/Curing**: Drag the "Curing Compound" to pulse a neon blue layer over the pavement, locking in the material.

### 5. STYLE & AESTHETIC GUIDE
*   **Visuals**: Use glowing, high-contrast neon accents. The construction site background should be a sleek, top-down isometric view on a dark grid.
*   **Animations**: All interactions must involve GSAP-like easing (or CSS `transition: all 0.3s cubic-bezier`).
*   **Callouts**: Use leader lines to label layers (e.g., "Thickness = 250mm"). When a user hovers over a label, zoom the canvas by 120% to focus on the layer's cross-section.

### 6. DELIVERY CONSTRAINTS
*   **No Clutter**: Absolutely no "Exam Paper" layouts or lists of questions. This is a **Construction Simulator**, not a digital document.
*   **Code Structure**: Provide a clean, modular structure with `CSS` at the top, `HTML` in the middle, and a single, well-commented `JavaScript` block at the bottom.
*   **Performance**: Ensure high frame rates during dragging and animation.
