IRC:58-2015 Guidelines Manual

1. Contraction Joints (Clause 11.2)

Contraction joints must be provided to control temperature contraction and warping. Recommended spacing $L$ should not exceed $4.5\text{ m}$ for Plain Jointed Concrete Pavement (Pavement Quality Concrete - PQC).

IRC:58 Design Formula:
Allowable Contraction Joint Spacing, $L_{max} = 4.5\text{ m}$ (for slab thickness $\ge 150\text{ mm}$)
Required Saw-Cut Depth: $h / 4$ to $h / 3$ (Typically $1/3$ of the slab thickness).

2. Load Transfer System: Dowel Bars

Dowel bars are smooth steel bars placed across transverse joints to transfer high shear loads between adjacent slabs without restricting horizontal thermal movement.

Dowel Bar Properties for Slab Thickness of $250\text{ mm}$:
- Recommended Diameter: $32\text{ mm}$
- Minimum Length: $450\text{ mm}$ (typically spaced at $300\text{ mm}$ center-to-center)
- Standard coating: Bond-preventing lubricant on one half to allow free joint movement.

3. Joint Cutting Timeline Analysis

Saw cutting must take place in the critical hardening window (typically 4 to 12 hours after concrete laying depending on ambient temperature). Cutting too early induces raveling; cutting too late causes uncontrolled shrinkage cracks elsewhere.
