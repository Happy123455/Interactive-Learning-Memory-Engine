Concept Highlight: Why do Angle Struts buckle eccentrically?
Because equal angles are asymmetrical about standard coordinate axes, their principal axes (U and V) are tilted at 45°. An eccentricity in standard X or Y directions induces bi-axial moments that trigger immediate lateral-torsional bending and flexural buckling long before the theoretical concentric Euler limit is reached! This interactive shows how eccentricity collapses the safe operating envelope.
