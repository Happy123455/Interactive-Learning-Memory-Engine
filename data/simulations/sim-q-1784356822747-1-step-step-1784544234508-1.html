Step 2: Equivalent Bending Moment

As per IS 456:2000 Clause 41.4.2, the design moment envelope must incorporate torsion as an equivalent bending moment.

Given Parameters:
$M$ = 40.0 kN·m
$T$ = 15.0 kN·m
$D$ = 500 mm
$b$ = 300 mm
2.1 Calculate Torsion Equivalent ($M_t$)
$$M_t = T \cdot \left[ \frac{1 + (D/b)}{1.7} \right]$$
$$M_t = 15 \cdot \left[ \frac{1 + (500/300)}{1.7} \right] = 23.53\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$$
2.2 Calculate Combined Total ($M_e$)
$$M_e = M + M_t$$
$$M_e = 40.00 + 23.53 = 63.53\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$$
