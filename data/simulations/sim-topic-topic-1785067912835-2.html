IS 800 : 2007 SECTION 7

Compressive Strength Reference Guidelines

Clause 7.1.1 - Design Compressive Strength

The design compressive strength, Pd, of a member is calculated using:

Pd = Ae × fcd

Where:
• Ae = Effective cross-sectional area of the compression member.
• fcd = Design compressive stress, determined based on the buckling class of the section, slenderness ratio, and yield strength of steel.

Clause 7.1.2 - Maximum Slenderness Limits

The slenderness ratio of a compression member, computed as KL / r, must not exceed critical limits:

Member Type Max λ
Members carrying compressive loads resulting from dead & imposed loads 180
Members subjected to compression forces from wind/earthquake actions 250

Clause 7.1.2.1 - Calculation of fcd

The design compressive stress, fcd, is computed using the Merchant-Rankine formula:

fcd = χ × fy / γm0
• Reduction factor, χ = 1 / [ φ + (φ² - λbar²)0.5 ] ≤ 1.0 • φ = 0.5 × [ 1 + α × (λbar - 0.2) + λbar² ] • Non-dimensional slenderness ratio, λbar = (fy / fcc)0.5 • Euler buckling stress, fcc = π² × E / (KL/r)²
