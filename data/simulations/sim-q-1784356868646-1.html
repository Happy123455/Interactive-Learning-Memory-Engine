Welcome to PaveEval ELI5 Sandbox! 🚚💨

This sandbox simplifies complex Pavement Evaluation Science into playful ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5) concepts!


🌟 How to Play & Learn:

  • 🏎️ Drive the Squeaky Truck: Slide your mouse/pointer left and right over the pavement canvas to drive a squeaky truck. Look at the wavy dynamic deformation under its wheels!
  • 🦖 Drag Sandbox Toys: Drag Dino-Weights, Rainy Clouds, or Bumpy Biscuits from the left menu and drop them anywhere on the road canvas.
  • 🎛️ Double Sandbox Modes: Switch modes to focus on Structural Capacity (bending bones) vs Functional Ride (bumpy tummies & water sliding).
  • 🧩 Solve Diagnostics: Match up the cards in the third column. Correct matches trigger playful speech synthesizers explaining engineering math like a cozy storybook.

🔊 Enable Audio using the top bar toggle to hear the structural hum, squeaks, and diagnostics!
