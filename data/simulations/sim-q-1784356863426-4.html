Engineering Lab Guidelines

Welcome to the Flexible vs. Rigid Pavement Diagnostic System. Your task is to investigate the stress propagation and layer mechanics of these two core infrastructure pavements.

Step 1: Classification Phase
Drag parameters from the bank below and drop them into the empty classification slots on the sandbox dashboards. You must pair all parameters to their scientifically accurate pavement design pattern.

Step 2: Load Simulation Phase
Once both structural models are fully classified, the Axle Simulation will unlock. Run the wheel loading stress test to trace how physical pressure attenuates through depth.

Step 3: Advanced Diagnostics
Enable Microscope mode to hover your pointer over the base boundary and physically examine dynamic micro-aggregate point interactions!
