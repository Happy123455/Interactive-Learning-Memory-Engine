Single Angle Struts

Compression members consisting of a single angle connected by one bolt at each end suffer from secondary eccentricities. The axial force is transferred eccentric to both principal axes.

Slenderness $\lambda$

The slenderness ratio $\lambda = KL/r_{vv}$ controls Euler buckling. Angles buckle about their weakest axis (v-v axis). IS 800 specifies a limit of $180$ for structural members subjected to compressive loads.

Combined Stresses

Eccentric connection causes a bending moment $M = P \cdot e$. The interaction ratio checks the structural system against combined direct compression and bending capacity: $\frac{P}{P_d} + \frac{M}{M_{dz}} \le 1.0$.
