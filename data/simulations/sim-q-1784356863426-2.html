Knowledge Base

What is ESWL?

Equivalent Single Wheel Load is the single load that creates an identical stress value at a target subgrade depth as a complex dual-tire configuration.

Boyd & Foster Method

A standard semi-logarithmic design framework that scales ESWL based on the clear spacing (d) and overall spacing (S) thresholds.

Stress Bulbs

Concentric pressure bulbs represent overlapping load distributions. At deep horizons, these separate stresses combine completely.
