Welcome! Laced columns use diagonal plates (lacings) to connect individual components—like our two ISMC 350 channels—so they act as a single, strong unit.

To design these lacing plates, our very first step is to find the transverse shear force (Vt) they must resist.

According to IS 800, this transverse shear is calculated as a specific percentage of the axial load (P). Do you recall what this percentage is?
