Clause 3.2: Equivalent Standard Axle (ESAL) Computation

The design traffic is considered in terms of standard axle load of 80 kN. All dynamic wheel distributions are converted into Standard Axle repetitions using the Axle Load Equivalency Factor (LEF).

Clause 4.1: Subgrade Characterization & CBR

Subgrade soils must be evaluated using California Bearing Ratio (CBR) tests at the worst moisture condition (soaking method). In situations with high water tables, corrective granular drains are mandatory.

Clause 5.2: Multi-layer Stress Analysis Criteria

Flexible pavements disperses stress to native layers by utilizing depth distribution. Minimum thicknesses of dynamic surfacing materials prevent structural cracking and rutting shear zones.
