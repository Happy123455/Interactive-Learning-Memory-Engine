Contraction Joint Mechanics

Controls transverse cracking caused by concrete shrinkage and thermal cooling. The saw-cut notch acts as a stress riser, forcing the crack to occur cleanly along the joint line.

Expansion Joint Mechanics

Accommodates positive thermal expansion during summer conditions to prevent crushing or blowout failures. Features a full-depth compressible filler board and expansion cap on dowel bars.

Warping & Longitudinal Joints

Relieves stresses caused by moisture/temperature differentials (curling/warping). Deformed tie bars anchor adjacent lanes tightly together to prevent joint opening.
