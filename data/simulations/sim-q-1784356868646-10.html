LAB GUIDE & CHEAT SHEET

1. Spot Speed Percentiles

The Spot Speed Distribution curve uses dynamic calculations of vehicular movement:

  • 85th Percentile Speed: The upper speed limit at which 85% of traffic is safely traveling. Typical speed limit design baseline.
  • 98th Percentile Speed: Geometric speed baseline used directly in determining curve parameters, super-elevation, and sight distances.
  • 15th Percentile Speed: Typical lower limit constraint baseline.

2. Time Mean Speed (Vt) vs Space Mean Speed (Vs)

Are they identical? No! Time Mean Speed represents the mathematical mean of all vehicle speeds recorded at a cross-section over an observation period. Space Mean Speed is the harmonic mean of the speeds over a given segment length. Mathematical proof confirms that Vt ≥ Vs always.

3. PCU (Passenger Car Unit)

Different vehicles occupy varying quantities of space and move differently. To maintain uniform traffic flow analyses, engineers convert all vehicle types into an equivalent number of passenger cars (PCUs). Try dropping them on the scale to observe structural transformations!
