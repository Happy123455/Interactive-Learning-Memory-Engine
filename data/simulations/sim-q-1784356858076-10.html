Transportation Materials: Bitumen Ductility Standard

The Ductility Test determines the tensile performance and adhesive elasticity of asphalt binders/bitumen. Under standard conditions, a dog-bone shaped briquette is stretched horizontally in a water bath at a fixed temperature and rate.

Standard Conditions (ASTM D113)

  • Standard Temperature: \(25.0^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}\)
  • Standard Pulling Velocity: \(5.0\text{ cm/min} \pm 5\%\)
  • Rupture Length: The distance (in cm) the carriage travels before the thin thread of bitumen breaks. For premium bitumen, typical values exceed \(100\text{ cm}\).

How to operate the simulation:

  1. Set the water bath Temperature and Pull Speed. (Warning: Deviations from standard values significantly alter viscosity, elasticity, and total ductility).
  2. Toggle Time Warp to speed up wait times (standard test takes up to 20 minutes!).
  3. Click Start Test. Observe the rising force values as the specimen resists initial deformation, followed by a force decline as visual necking and extreme thinning occur.
  4. Hover over the machine parts inside the bath to review mechanics.

Physical Principles Simulated

The mechanical tensile tension profile models a viscoelastic material: initial elastic response peaks quickly, yielding to plastic thinning. Lower temperatures dramatically increase peak tensile forces but yield a highly brittle, earlier rupture.
