Hydrology Core Concept

How does a Lysimeter capture ET?

A Lysimeter physical scale isolated system permits the direct calculation of total ET based on Mass Balance principles over time:

ET = ΔS + I - D

Where ΔS is change in storage (weight difference), I is inflow water, and D is gravity-drained surplus water.

Exam Tip
When soil moisture falls below the Permanent Wilting Point (15% in this model), plant water tension is too great, guard cells turgidity drops, stomata close, and T drops to zero. • Lysimeters measure actual ET directly by mass balance without physical disturbance. • PET is atmosphere's pure demand; AET is the actual hydrological volume released. • High wind increases boundary layer shear, spiking evaporation from leaves.
